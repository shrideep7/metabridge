The lifecycle

One governed lifecycle, end to end

The whole modernization program — not just conversion. Every stage runs on the same platform, over the same canonical models. A single conversion job is one pass through Assess → Design → Migrate → Validate.

01 ASSESS

Map the estate

Discover a Digital Twin of the whole estate; score AI-readiness, technical debt, cloud cost and security posture.

02 DESIGN

Semantic intelligence

Lift platform-specific logic into the canonical CIR — intent, not syntax — and plan governed pipelines.

03 MIGRATE

Target-native output

Generate target-native pipelines and assets across 18 source formats, with AI review and approve-and-apply.

04 VALIDATE

Prove equivalence

Confidence scoring, column-level lineage and reconciliation — schema, semantics and business rules.

05 GOVERN

Evidence & approval

Classify PII/PHI, apply residency & masking policy, and capture a tamper-evident audit trail with human approval.

06 OPERATE

Run & observe

Monitor pipeline, agent and connector health, SLA and alerts — and extend the platform via the marketplace.

Governed agentic AI

12 agents that propose. A human that approves. A record that can't be forged.

MetaBridge's agents collaborate over the shared CIR — but every AI action is scored from real evidence, held for a separate human approver when consequential, and written to a tamper-evident audit chain. Deterministic where it matters; the LLM assist is advisory-only and off by default. This is the trust difference a regulated client's risk team will accept.

Deployment & trust

Enterprise-grade, inside your boundary.

Single-tenant & self-hosted

Run MetaBridge in customer-controlled infrastructure or a dedicated VPC — data never leaves the boundary. Docker and systemd paths documented.

Role-based access & SoD

Owner, admin, engineer and viewer roles enforced server-side across console and API — with segregation of duties: the requester of a consequential action can't approve it.

Tamper-evident audit

An HMAC-keyed audit chain, re-verified on read, records who did what, at what confidence, and who approved it — across every run.

Secrets & signing

Write-only secret storage kept out of manifests and generated assets; marketplace packages are Ed25519-signed and verified before install.

Private model access

Approved AI endpoints only — Anthropic API or Amazon Bedrock via the server's IAM role. LLM assist is advisory-only and off by default.

Feature flags & versioning

Deterministic capability gating (enable / role / rollout), a component version registry and a plugin registry — govern what runs, where.

Governance

Compliance isn't a slide. It's in the pipeline.

MetaBridge classifies columns against GDPR (incl. Art. 9 special categories), CCPA/CPRA and HIPAA — including the SAP data dictionary — then applies your residency and masking policy on every conversion and scaffold, and captures the evidence in a tamper-evident audit trail. It provides compliance mapping and audit evidence, not a certification.
